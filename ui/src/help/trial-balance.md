# Trial balance

Every account's total debits and credits. In double-entry bookkeeping **each currency must net to zero**; if it does not, money was created or lost somewhere.

![The trial balance](trial-balance.png)

- The totals line shows debits and credits per currency. A red banner appears if they differ.
- **As of** shows the books at a past moment; leave it empty for now.
- **Include empty accounts** also lists accounts with no movement.
- The sidebar **Product** filter also applies.
- Click a line to open that account.
